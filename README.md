# 🎙️ Speeky

> A personalized app built with love for my amazing girlfriend to help her practice, refine, and level up her communication and public speaking skills ❤️

---

## 💡 What is Speeky?

Speeky is a dedicated tool I created to help my girlfriend train her oratorical and communication skills in a comfortable, stress-free environment. 

Whether it's practicing speech pacing, working through presentation ideas, or building confidence when speaking out loud, Speeky gives her a space designed just for her to practice and get better every day.
